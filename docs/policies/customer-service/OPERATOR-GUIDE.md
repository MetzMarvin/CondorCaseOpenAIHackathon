## GOV-001 · Apply the correct authority

Trigger: For each case.

1. Use the law that applies to the event date and the route.
2. Apply binding law before a fare term or an internal rule.
3. Use Court of Justice interpretations when you apply EU law.
4. Do not treat an interpretative notice as binding legislation.
5. Use a fare term only after you check the contract version.
6. If sources conflict, send the conflict to Legal.
7. Continue clear assistance duties during the legal review.
8. Do not use this draft as an approved Condor policy.

Do not reduce a legal right to meet an internal budget.

Source: [EU261](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/261/oj/eng), Article 15.
